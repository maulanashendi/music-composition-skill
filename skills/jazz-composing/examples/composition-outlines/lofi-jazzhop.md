# lofi-jazzhop — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 86 BPM  
**Key:** D minor  
**Form:** Intro – Loop A – A′ – Breakdown – Lift – Dissolve – Outro

**Harmony:** Main loop Dm9 – Gm11 – Bbmaj7 – A7alt; A′ introduces Dm9 – Bbmaj7 – Ebmaj7#11 – A7alt; held upper voices and occasional Gm11 – G#dim7 – Am9 provide variation without abandoning the short loop.

**Hook:** Two-bar falling-third motif landing on the “and” of beat 2, answered one octave away; an alternate hook holds a top color tone while the harmony changes beneath it.

**Bass behavior:** Fat root-and-fifth anchor, sparse approach notes, selected kick echoes, and occasional chromatic walkups under a held Rhodes voicing.

**Drum intent:** Dusty kick, laid-back 2-and-4 snare, sparse hats expanding into quiet swung-sixteenth ghosts; lift remains restrained.

**Arrangement development:** Build the same loop through filtering, subtraction, addition, and one reharm; lead enters late; breakdown returns to keys and bass; lift is the fullest pass; dissolve removes parts one by one.

**Ending:** Remove drums and lead, let bass stop first, and leave the last Dm9 top voice hanging in the room tone.
