# fusion-vamp — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 108 BPM  
**Key:** E minor  
**Form:** Bass Riff Intro – Vamp A – Build – Breakdown – Solo/Peak – Release – Final Vamp – Stop

**Harmony:** Em9-centered static vamp with Em9 – A13 turnaround, Ebmaj7#11 chromatic stab, and occasional Gsus13 – G13 release.

**Hook:** Angular syncopated bass-and-lead unison riff plus an offbeat full-band upper-structure stab.

**Bass behavior:** Short electric riff that defines the piece, tightly doubles kick, uses pickups into beat 1, and remains exposed during breakdown.

**Drum intent:** Hard on-grid 2-and-4 backbeat, straight sixteenth hats, kick doubling the riff, ghost snares and openings added during builds.

**Arrangement development:** Bass states riff alone; drums lock in; keys and lead add layers; build raises register and adds stabs; breakdown removes nearly everything; full band re-enters on a downbeat; solo peaks over the vamp; final statement is concise.

**Ending:** Play the riff one final time and stop on a short unison Em9 hit.
