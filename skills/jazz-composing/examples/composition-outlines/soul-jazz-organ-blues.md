# soul-jazz-organ-blues — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 104 BPM  
**Key:** F blues / major  
**Form:** Organ Vamp Intro – 12-Bar Head – Organ Solo – Sax/Guitar Solo – Head Return – Outro

**Harmony:** Gospel-blues twelve-bar form using F7, Bb7, Bbdim7, D7#9, Gm7, and C7#9; use Bb/F – F7 plagal color and a sparse turnaround rather than long ii–V chains.

**Hook:** Organ root-fifth pedal vamp, followed by a two-bar horn riff answered by a two-bar gospel organ fill.

**Bass behavior:** Legato low drawbar or pedal-bass line, locked to kick, mostly roots and fifths, with passing notes added only as the organ solo peaks.

**Drum intent:** Swung ride, heavy snare behind 2 and 4, kick supporting organ pedal, and increasingly sloshy hi-hat through the solo arc.

**Arrangement development:** Organ opens with slow Leslie; drums and guitar enter; horns state the head; organ solo grows through drawbars and Leslie speed; sax or guitar takes foreground while organ returns to comping; head returns with the full band.

**Ending:** Sustain F7#9 or F6/9, add an upward organ palm smear, and slow the Leslie back down.
