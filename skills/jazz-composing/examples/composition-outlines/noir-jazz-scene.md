# noir-jazz-scene — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 46 BPM  
**Key:** A minor  
**Form:** Scene 1: Alleyway – Scene 2: Shadow – Scene 3: Confession – Scene 4: Fog – Scene 5: Resolution

**Harmony:** Hold Am(maj7) for extended decay; move through Am(maj7) – Am7 – Am6 – Fmaj7#11 – E7(b9,b13); confession briefly shifts to Cm9 – Fm9 – Abmaj7#11 – G7alt before returning.

**Hook:** Three-note muted-trumpet call bending into b3 or b5, answered by one low piano cluster after a long silence.

**Bass behavior:** One dark upright pluck every one or two bars, slightly behind the brush, then complete absence in the Fog scene.

**Drum intent:** Near-silent brush swirl as the only grid reference, optional felt-kick shadows, no conventional backbeat, and no loud energy state.

**Arrangement development:** Bass and one decaying chord establish the alley; brush and muted trumpet enter for the shadow; tenor replaces trumpet in confession; fog removes drums and bass; resolution returns the ensemble without exceeding pp.

**Ending:** Apply molto ritardando, state one final trumpet fragment, play one bass pluck, and let Am(maj7) decay into total silence.
