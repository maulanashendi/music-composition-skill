# hiphop-jazz-boombap — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 86 BPM  
**Key:** D minor  
**Form:** Filtered Intro – Verse 1 – Hook – Verse 2 – Breakdown – Final Hook – Filtered Outro

**Harmony:** Four-bar rootless Rhodes loop Dm9 – G13 – Cmaj9 – A7(b9,b13); verse variation may use Fmaj9 – Em7 – Dm9 – Cmaj9 while sub-bass retains root authority.

**Hook:** Short muted-trumpet or tenor phrase at the end of the loop, supported by an offbeat vocal-chop accent.

**Bass behavior:** Sustained sub-bass roots with clean cutoffs, selective lock to slightly early kicks, and space around the dragged snare.

**Drum intent:** Regular-time boom-bap with kick slightly ahead, snare behind beats 2 and 4, swung hats with uneven velocity, and ghost notes around the backbeat; beat-3 snare is reserved for an explicitly labeled half-time section.

**Arrangement development:** Filtered sample opens; drums and bass enter together; hook adds horn and chops; brief drum drops occur every eight bars; verse 2 changes bass texture; breakdown strips to sample, bass, and one solo voice; final hook restores layers.

**Ending:** Re-engage the low-pass filter, remove drums, and let the loop cut off rather than resolving functionally.
