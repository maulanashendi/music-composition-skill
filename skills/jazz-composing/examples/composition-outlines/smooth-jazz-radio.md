# smooth-jazz-radio — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 90 BPM  
**Key:** C major  
**Form:** Intro – Verse – Pre-Chorus – Chorus – Verse 2 – Pre-Chorus – Chorus – Bridge/Solo – Final Chorus – Outro

**Harmony:** Verse uses Cmaj9 – G/B – Am11 – C/G – Fmaj7 – Dm7/G; pre-chorus leans on smooth slash chords; chorus resolves cleanly to Cmaj9; bridge briefly colors G major before returning through Dm7/G.

**Hook:** Vocal-style sax or clean-guitar phrase with a scoop into the target and vibrato only near the end of a held note.

**Bass behavior:** Legato fretless-style line with stepwise descent, precise kick alignment, and supportive slides that never become the primary riff.

**Drum intent:** Straight precise sixteenths, cross-stick in verses, light R&B kick syncopation, and a layered full snare in choruses.

**Arrangement development:** Guitar or e-piano and pad open; verse lead stays low; pre-chorus raises contour; chorus raises lead register and changes snare timbre; bridge solo remains melodic and hook-adjacent; final chorus is the fullest polished texture.

**Ending:** Repeat the chorus hook under short vocal-style ad-libs and fade without harmonic disruption.
