# neo-soul-midnight — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 74 BPM  
**Key:** F major  
**Form:** Intro – A1 – A2 – Bridge – Peak – Acceptance – Outro

**Harmony:** Pedal-F intro using Fmaj9 – Fm(maj9) – Ebmaj9/F; A sections move through Fmaj9 – Am7 – Dm9 – Gm11 with a Bbm7 – Eb7 backdoor; peak resolves Gm11 – C7alt – Fmaj9; outro settles Bbmaj7#11 – Fmaj9.

**Hook:** Syncopated pickup rising a sixth, held across the barline, then settling to the third; later returns in a new register or over a reharmonized bass.

**Bass behavior:** Warm sustained foundation, pedal F in the intro, selective anticipations before section arrivals, and slightly greater activity toward the peak.

**Drum intent:** Sparse kick, snare behind 2 and 4, hats growing from quarters to low-velocity swung-sixteenth ghosts; bridge reduces drums before the peak fill.

**Arrangement development:** Rhodes and bass open; lead and drums enter gradually; A2 adds chromatic reharmonization; bridge removes density; peak uses the fullest register; acceptance strips layers back.

**Ending:** Fragment the hook in a lower register, use a plagal Bbmaj7#11 – Fmaj9 settle, and leave the final Rhodes voicing to decay.
