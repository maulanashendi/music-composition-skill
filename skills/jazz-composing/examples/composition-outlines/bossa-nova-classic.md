# bossa-nova-classic — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 120 BPM  
**Key:** C major  
**Form:** Guitar Intro – A1 – A2 – B – A3 – Solo – A4 – Tag

**Harmony:** Jobim-style chromatic movement Cmaj7 – C#dim7 – Dm7 – D#dim7 – C/E – A7(b9,b13); bridge uses Fmaj7 – G7(13) – Gm9 – C7(b9); return lands on C6/9.

**Hook:** One-note melody held while harmony moves, alternating with two bars of lead silence answered by denser guitar batida.

**Bass behavior:** Short acoustic roots on beat 1, fifth or chord tone near beat 3, and anticipations into changes.

**Drum intent:** Straight shaker sixteenths, cross-stick echoing batida, light surdo pulse, and no swing in guitar, bass, or percussion.

**Arrangement development:** Nylon guitar opens; shaker and bass enter in stages; lead enters sotto voce; A2 adds counter-melody and soft pad; bridge increases harmonic motion; A3 is fullest; solo returns to verse changes; outro strips down.

**Ending:** Repeat the final two bars three times and leave a strummed C6/9 ringing.
