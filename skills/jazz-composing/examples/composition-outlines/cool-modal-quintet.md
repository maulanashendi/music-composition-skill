# cool-modal-quintet — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 112 BPM  
**Key:** D Dorian  
**Form:** Pedal Intro – Head – Solo 1 – Eb-Dorian Bridge – Solo 2 – Head Return – Outro

**Harmony:** Dm11 and So What voicings over a D pedal; bridge shifts one semitone to Ebm11 and returns directly to D Dorian without a functional dominant cadence.

**Hook:** Three-note motif emphasizing the natural sixth, followed by one or two bars of silence; each return changes rhythmic placement or the final note.

**Bass behavior:** Sustained D pedal in the head, sparse modal motion during solos, and a slightly behind-the-brush placement; optional pianoless arrangement keeps the same bass role.

**Drum intent:** Continuous soft brush swish, barely audible kick, gentle hi-hat on 2 and 4, and no hard accents; energy comes from horn counterpoint.

**Arrangement development:** Bass and brushes establish space; sparse piano quartal punctuation enters; lead states the motif; second horn adds counterpoint; bridge lifts the mode by a semitone; solos develop motifs; the head returns thinner.

**Ending:** Reduce the head to one suspended Harmon-muted note over Dm11 and let the ensemble finish morendo.
