# classic-jazz-swing — Canonical Composition Outline

**Tempo:** 148 BPM  
**Key:** F major  
**Form:** Roll-off – Head A1 – Head A2 – Reed Soli – Solo Break – Solo – Shout Chorus – Tag

**Harmony:** Functional swing changes centered on F6, Bb7, Gm7, and C7; use F6 – F#dim7 – Gm7 – C7 and a rhythm-changes turnaround; land on sixth chords rather than dense modern extensions.

**Hook:** Two-bar staccato brass riff answered by two bars of legato reeds; solo section opens with an unaccompanied chromatic enclosure lick.

**Bass behavior:** Four quarter-note walking line, slightly ahead, with chromatic approaches and silence under ensemble hits.

**Drum intent:** Ride-led swing, feathered four-beat kick, hi-hat on 2 and 4, press rolls into arrivals, and snare accents doubling shout-chorus hits.

**Arrangement development:** Roll-off introduces the band; reeds lead the head, brass answers; soli tightens the texture; solo break creates contrast; backgrounds accumulate beneath the solo; shout chorus is the dynamic and density peak.

**Ending:** Hold a dominant sonority with section vibrato, pause, then finish on a short full-band F6 hit.
